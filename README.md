# Skincare Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze skincare sales performance across different markets, countries, product categories, and time periods.

The analysis focuses on sales, quantity sold, profit, discounts, product performance, and market-level trends to identify business patterns and areas requiring attention.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Analysis

The Sales Analysis dashboard provides an overview of the business performance through key metrics and visualizations.

Key metrics include:

- Total Sales: $1.77M
- Total Quantity Sold: 75K
- Total Profit: $318.67K
- Monthly sales trends
- Average discount by market
- Total profit by market
- Profit by product category
- Sales by market

### 2. Product Analysis

### 3. Insights & Recommendations

The final dashboard page summarizes key findings from the analysis and presents business recommendations.

Key observations include:

- Europe records the highest profit while having the lowest average discount.
- Asia Pacific has the highest average discount but ranks lower in profit.
- Body care generates the highest sales among the product categories.
- Face care records the lowest sales among the categories shown.
- Turkey and Nigeria appear among the least profitable countries.
- Sales show noticeable peaks during April and December, with lower sales in some months such as July.

The dashboard also provides recommendations relating to discount strategy, product performance, country-level profitability, and seasonal sales planning.

## Key Business Questions

This analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

- How much total sales and profit were generated?
- Which markets generate the highest profit?
- Which markets receive the highest discounts?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which countries are the most and least profitable?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- How does sales performance change throughout the year?
- Where could the business improve its pricing, discount, and marketing strategies?
